# Incident context

Add this playbook when the target looks defensive or reactive. Each investigator runs these searches inside its own source, around the date the target appeared.

- **Git**: messages like "fix", "hotfix", "revert", "add check", or a revert followed by "re-apply with". Same-day fixes after a complaint are a strong signal.
- **Gmail**: customer complaints, chargeback or dispute notices, courier or payment-gateway incident emails, and platform outage notices in the days before the change.
- **Slack**: "broken", "down", "not working", "refund", or the error text, in the days before the change.
- **Meeting notes**: calls scheduled at short notice with a freelancer or vendor near the change date.
- **Store data**: a spike in refunds, cancellations, failed payments, or unfulfilled orders before the change.
- **Documents**: a written post-mortem or a "what went wrong" note, even an informal one.
