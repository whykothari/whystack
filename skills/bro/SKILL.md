Say your last message again, the way one person talks to another.

- Put the one thing I must know or do in the first line.
- Use plain words. Give the full form of each abbreviation, or drop it.
- Cut it to half the length or less. Keep every fact, number, path, and command that I need.
- Keep the active output style.
