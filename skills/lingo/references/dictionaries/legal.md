# Legal (business and commercial)

This file is vocabulary for prompts, not legal advice.

Business law that a founder or an in-house legal team meets: drafting and negotiating contracts, entity and financing work, IP ownership, privacy compliance, employment basics, and disputes. Commercial counsel, in-house lawyers, contract managers, and founders use these terms. Most terms follow US practice. Privacy terms also follow the EU GDPR.

## Terms

### Contract drafting and structure

| Term | Plain meaning | Use it when | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Defined terms | Capitalized words that have one fixed meaning in the contract | You draft or review any agreement | core |
| Covenant | A promise to do or not do something | You state an ongoing obligation | core |
| Representations and warranties (reps and warranties) | Statements of fact that a party promises are true | You allocate risk for facts at signing | core |
| Efforts standard (best efforts, commercially reasonable efforts) | How hard a party must try to get a result | The result is not fully in that party's control | expert |
| Material breach | Serious failure that lets the other party end the deal | You set termination rights | core |
| Cure period | Time a party gets to fix a breach | You draft termination for cause | core |
| Term and termination | How long the deal lasts and how it can end | You set the contract length and exits | core |
| Termination for convenience | Right to end the deal without a reason | A party needs a flexible exit | core |
| Auto-renewal (evergreen clause) | Term renews by itself unless a party gives notice | You set renewal and notice dates | core |
| Survival clause | Lists the terms that stay in force after the deal ends | You protect duties after termination | expert |
| Assignment clause | Says if a party may transfer the contract to another | A merger or sale of a party is possible | core |
| Entire agreement (integration clause) | Signed text replaces all earlier promises and drafts | You block claims based on side promises | core |
| Order of precedence | Says which document controls when terms conflict | An MSA sits above SOWs or order forms | core |
| Boilerplate (general provisions) | Standard closing clauses found in most contracts | You review notices, assignment, and governing law | core |
| Redline | Marked draft that shows each proposed change | You negotiate a draft with the other side | core |

### Risk allocation

| Term | Plain meaning | Use it when | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Indemnification (indemnity) | Promise to cover the other party's losses from set claims | You allocate the risk of third-party claims | core |
| Duty to defend | Duty to run and pay for the other party's defense | You draft or narrow an indemnity | expert |
| IP indemnity | Vendor covers claims that its product infringes IP | You buy or sell software or content | core |
| Limitation of liability (LoL) | Clause that limits what a party can owe | You set the maximum exposure | core |
| Liability cap (general cap) | Maximum amount a party must pay for most claims | You set a dollar or fees-based limit | core |
| Super cap (increased cap) | Higher cap for set risks such as data breaches | A risk needs more cover than the general cap | expert |
| Carve-outs (uncapped claims) | Claims that the cap or damages waiver does not limit | You list risks with no limit | core |
| Consequential damages (indirect damages) | Losses that follow indirectly from a breach, such as lost profits | You draft a damages waiver | core |
| Liquidated damages | Fixed sum agreed in advance for a set breach | Actual loss is hard to measure | expert |
| Disclaimer of warranties | States that no promises apply beyond those listed | You sell a product "as is" | core |
| Force majeure | Excuses performance for events outside a party's control | You plan for disasters, wars, or outages | core |

### Commercial agreements

| Term | Plain meaning | Use it when | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| MSA (master services agreement) | Framework terms that govern many later orders or SOWs | You set up a long vendor or customer relationship | core |
| SOW (statement of work) | Scope, deliverables, timeline, and fees for one project | You define services under an MSA | core |
| Order form | Short document with product, quantity, price, and term | You sell SaaS on standard terms | core |
| NDA (non-disclosure agreement), mutual or one-way | Contract to keep shared information secret | You share confidential information before a deal | core |
| Confidential information | Information that the contract protects from disclosure | You define what an NDA covers | core |
| Residuals clause | Lets the recipient use what stays in unaided memory | A party needs freedom to build similar products | expert |
| DPA (data processing agreement) | Contract that sets how a vendor handles personal data | A vendor processes personal data for you | core |
| SLA (service level agreement) | Measurable service promises, such as uptime, with remedies | You buy or sell a hosted service | core |
| Terms of service (ToS) | Standard terms users accept to use a product | You launch a product or app | core |
| Term sheet (LOI, letter of intent) | Mostly non-binding outline of key deal terms | You agree the main points before full drafting | core |
| Contract playbook | Approved position and fallbacks for each clause | You standardize how the team negotiates | core |

### Corporate and financing

| Term | Plain meaning | Use it when | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| C corporation (C corp) | Company taxed apart from its owners | You form a startup that will raise venture money | core |
| LLC (limited liability company) | Flexible entity with liability shield and pass-through tax | You form a small or closely held business | core |
| Certificate of incorporation (charter) | Filed document that creates a corporation and sets shares | You form or amend a corporation | core |
| Bylaws | Internal rules for how a corporation is run | You set board and stockholder procedures | core |
| Operating agreement | Ownership and governance rules for an LLC | You form an LLC or add members | core |
| Board consent (unanimous written consent) | Board approval signed in writing without a meeting | You approve a corporate action | core |
| Cap table (capitalization table) | Record of who owns which shares and options | You raise money or grant equity | core |
| Founder vesting (restricted stock) | Founder shares earned over time, with buyback on exit | You issue founder stock | core |
| 83(b) election | IRS filing within 30 days to pay tax at grant | Someone receives restricted stock | core |
| SAFE (simple agreement for future equity) | Investment that converts to shares in a later priced round | You raise pre-seed or seed money | core |
| Valuation cap | Highest valuation at which a SAFE or note converts | You set SAFE or note terms | core |
| Due diligence | Investor or buyer review of the company's legal facts | You prepare for a financing or sale | core |

### Intellectual property

| Term | Plain meaning | Use it when | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| IP assignment | Transfer of IP ownership to another party | The company must own what people build | core |
| PIIA or CIIAA (invention assignment agreement) | Employee or contractor deal that assigns inventions and protects secrets | You hire anyone who creates work | core |
| Work made for hire | Work that the employer owns from the moment of creation | You commission copyrightable work | core |
| License grant (exclusive or non-exclusive) | Permission to use IP on stated terms | You let others use your IP | core |
| Background IP vs foreground IP | IP owned before the deal vs IP made under it | You set ownership in a services deal | expert |
| Trademark | Name or logo that identifies the source of goods | You choose or protect a brand name | core |
| Trademark clearance search | Check that a mark does not conflict with existing marks | Before you adopt a brand name | expert |
| Copyright | Protection for original works such as code and text | You protect content or software | core |
| Patent | Exclusive right to an invention for a set period | You protect a technical invention | core |
| Trade secret | Valuable information kept secret with reasonable measures | You protect know-how without registration | core |
| Open source license (permissive vs copyleft) | Terms for using open code; copyleft forces sharing changes | You ship code with third-party libraries | core |

### Privacy and data protection

| Term | Plain meaning | Use it when | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal data (personal information) | Information that relates to an identifiable person | You decide what a privacy law covers | core |
| Controller | Party that decides why and how data is processed | You assign GDPR roles | core |
| Processor | Party that processes data on a controller's behalf | You assign GDPR roles | core |
| Service provider (CCPA) | Vendor that processes data for a business under contract | You assign CCPA roles | expert |
| Subprocessor | A processor's vendor that also handles the data | You list vendors in a DPA | core |
| Lawful basis | GDPR legal ground for processing, such as consent or contract | You justify a processing activity | core |
| Legitimate interest | Lawful basis that weighs business need against user rights | You process data without consent | expert |
| Consent | Free, specific, informed agreement to processing | You rely on opt-in | core |
| DSAR (data subject access request) | A person's request to see, fix, or delete their data | You handle a user's data request | core |
| Privacy notice (privacy policy) | Public statement of what data you collect and why | You launch or change data use | core |
| DPIA (data protection impact assessment) | Risk review before high-risk processing starts | You launch risky data processing | expert |
| ROPA (record of processing activities) | Inventory of processing activities that GDPR requires | You document compliance | expert |
| Cross-border data transfer | Moving personal data out of a jurisdiction | You use vendors in other countries | core |
| SCCs (standard contractual clauses) | EU-approved contract terms for transfers outside the EEA | You send EU data to a non-EU vendor | core |
| Personal data breach notification | Duty to tell regulators and people about a breach | A security incident exposes personal data | core |
| Sale or sharing (CCPA) | Passing data for value or for cross-context ads | You use ad tech or data partners | expert |
| Sensitive data (special category data) | Higher-risk data such as health, race, or biometrics | You handle sensitive data | expert |
| Data minimization | Collect and keep only the data you need | You design data collection | expert |

### Employment law basics

| Term | Plain meaning | Use it when | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| At-will employment | Either side can end the job at any time | You draft US offers or terminations | core |
| Offer letter | Letter that sets role, pay, start date, and key terms | You hire an employee | core |
| Worker classification (employee vs contractor) | Legal test for whether a worker is an employee | You engage a contractor | core |
| Exempt vs non-exempt | Whether overtime rules apply to a role | You set pay for a role | core |
| Non-compete | Limit on work for a competitor after exit | You draft restrictive covenants | core |
| Non-solicitation | Ban on poaching staff or customers after exit | You protect the team and clients | core |
| Separation agreement (release of claims) | Severance paid in exchange for giving up claims | An employee leaves on negotiated terms | core |
| Protected class | Traits that anti-discrimination law protects | You review a hiring or firing decision | core |

### Disputes and compliance

| Term | Plain meaning | Use it when | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Governing law (choice of law) | Which jurisdiction's law interprets the contract | You draft the general provisions | core |
| Venue (forum selection) | Where disputes must be heard | You draft the dispute terms | core |
| Arbitration | Private, binding decision of a dispute outside court | You need private or faster resolution | core |
| Escalation clause | Steps to resolve a dispute before suit or arbitration | You set a dispute process | expert |
| Injunctive relief (equitable relief) | Court order to stop or force an act | Money cannot fix the harm, such as a leak | expert |
| Demand letter (cease and desist) | Formal letter that demands action before a lawsuit | You assert or answer a claim | core |
| Litigation hold (legal hold) | Order to keep documents that relate to a dispute | A lawsuit is likely | core |
| Attorney-client privilege | Protects confidential legal advice from disclosure | You share or label legal advice | core |
| Settlement agreement | Contract that ends a dispute on agreed terms | You resolve a claim | core |
| Anti-bribery laws (FCPA, UK Bribery Act) | Laws that ban bribes to officials and others | You sell abroad or use agents | expert |
| Export controls and sanctions (OFAC) | Rules that limit trade with set countries and parties | You sell or ship abroad | expert |

| who pays if we get sued | indemnification and duty to defend |
| how much we could lose at most | limitation of liability and liability cap |
| things that should not be capped | carve-outs from the cap (uncapped claims) |
| lost profits and knock-on losses | consequential damages waiver |
| promises about what is true | representations and warranties |
| the main contract plus project docs | MSA with SOWs, and an order of precedence clause |
| keep our stuff secret | mutual NDA with a defined purpose and term of confidentiality |
| vendor handles our customer data | DPA with subprocessor list and SCCs |
| uptime promise | SLA with service credits |
| get out of the deal | termination for convenience or for material breach with a cure period |
| it renews unless we cancel | auto-renewal with a non-renewal notice date |
| which state's law applies | governing law and venue |
| settle disputes without court | arbitration clause with an escalation step |
| make sure we own what contractors build | IP assignment and work made for hire language |
| employee agreement about inventions | PIIA (proprietary information and invention assignment agreement) |
| check if the brand name is free | trademark clearance search |
| using open source code safely | open source license review (permissive vs copyleft) |
| set up the company | incorporate a Delaware C corp: charter, bylaws, board consent, founder stock |
| raise money from angels | issue post-money SAFEs with a valuation cap |
| who owns what in the company | cap table |
| what data we collect and why | privacy notice and record of processing activities |
| user wants their data deleted | DSAR (data subject access request) response |
| is this contractor really an employee | worker classification analysis |
| stop someone from copying us | cease and desist letter, then injunctive relief |
| keep emails in case we get sued | litigation hold |
| investor checks our legal stuff | due diligence with a data room |

## Deliverable shapes

| Deliverable | Standard parts |
|---|---|
| NDA | parties, purpose, definition of confidential information, exclusions, permitted disclosures, term and term of confidentiality, return or destruction, remedies, governing law |
| MSA | definitions, services and SOW mechanics, fees and payment, term and termination, IP ownership, confidentiality, reps and warranties, disclaimer, indemnification, limitation of liability, insurance, general provisions, order of precedence |
| SOW | scope, deliverables, milestones and timeline, acceptance criteria, fees and payment schedule, assumptions, change control, key personnel |
| DPA | roles (controller, processor), subject matter and duration, data categories and data subjects, processing instructions, security measures, subprocessors, breach notice, assistance with requests, transfers and SCCs, audit, deletion at end |
| Contract review memo (issues list) | deal summary, key terms table, issues ranked by risk, proposed redline or fallback per issue, open business questions, recommendation |
| Term sheet | parties, deal structure, price or valuation, key economic terms, key legal terms, conditions, exclusivity, binding and non-binding parts, expiry |
| Terms of service | acceptance method, account rules, acceptable use, fees, IP and license, user content, disclaimers, limitation of liability, termination, dispute resolution, changes to terms |
| Privacy notice | controller identity, data collected, sources, purposes and lawful basis, sharing and recipients, transfers, retention, user rights and how to use them, contact details, effective date |
| Board consent | company name, recitals, resolutions, authorization of officers, effective date, director signatures |
| Demand letter | facts, legal basis of the claim, specific demand, deadline, consequences, reservation of rights |
| Contract playbook | clause list, standard position, acceptable fallbacks, walk-away points, approval owner per deviation |

## Misused or dated terms

| Term | Problem | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| Privacy Shield | Struck down in 2020; no longer a valid transfer basis | EU-US Data Privacy Framework or SCCs |
| PII (in GDPR work) | US term with a narrower scope than GDPR uses | personal data |
| Limited liability corporation | An LLC is not a corporation | limited liability company |
| 1099 employee | A worker is an employee or a contractor, not both | independent contractor |
| Best efforts (left undefined) | Courts read it in different ways | a defined efforts standard |
| Shall (for every verb) | Mixes duties, conditions, and statements | "must" for duties, "will" for future facts |
| Indemnify and hold harmless (as two duties) | Most courts treat the words as one duty | indemnify, defend, and state the scope |
| Consequential damages (as "all lost profits") | Lost profits can be direct damages | name the excluded loss types |
| Boilerplate (as "safe to skip") | Governing law and assignment change the deal | review the general provisions |
