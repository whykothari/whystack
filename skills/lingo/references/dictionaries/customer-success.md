# Customer success and support

The post-sale work of getting customers to value, keeping and growing them, and solving their problems at scale. Customer success managers, account managers, support agents and leads, CS and support ops, and founders who run post-sale teams use these terms.

## Terms

### Roles and coverage models

| Term | Plain meaning | Use it when | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| CSM (customer success manager) | Owner of a customer's results after the sale | You assign post-sale ownership | core |
| TAM (technical account manager) | Technical advisor assigned to large accounts | Accounts need deep product guidance | expert |
| Account manager (AM) | Owner of the commercial relationship and growth | You split commercial work from success work | core |
| Renewals manager | Specialist who runs contract renewals | You take renewal work off CSMs | expert |
| CS Ops (customer success operations) | Team that runs CS data, tools, and process | You build health scores or dashboards | expert |
| Book of business | Set of accounts that one CSM owns | You set CSM workload | core |
| Segmentation | Grouping customers by value or need to set coverage | You decide who gets which service | core |
| High-touch | One-to-one service from a named CSM | Accounts are large or complex | core |
| Low-touch | Light coverage, mostly one-to-many with some human contact | Mid-value accounts | core |
| Tech-touch (digital customer success) | Automated, scaled engagement with no named CSM | You serve many small accounts | core |
| Pooled CSM model | Shared team serves accounts instead of named owners | You scale coverage at low cost | expert |

### Onboarding and adoption

| Term | Plain meaning | Use it when | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sales-to-CS handoff | Transfer of deal context from sales to CS | You stop customers repeating themselves | core |
| Kickoff call | First post-sale meeting to agree goals and plan | You start onboarding | core |
| Onboarding | Getting a new customer set up and using the product | You plan the first weeks after signature | core |
| Implementation | Technical setup, configuration, and data migration | The product needs setup work | core |
| Go-live | Date the customer starts real use | You set onboarding deadlines | core |
| TTV (time to value) | Time from signature until the customer gets value | You judge onboarding speed | core |
| Activation | Customer completes the key first actions | You measure early success | expert |
| Adoption | How fully the customer uses the product | You track usage health | core |
| Customer enablement | Training that helps customer users work well with the product | You plan adoption work | core |
| Success plan | Shared plan of customer goals, milestones, and owners | You align on outcomes | core |
| Desired outcome | Business result the customer bought the product for | You define success for an account | expert |

### Account management and health

| Term | Plain meaning | Use it when | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Customer health score | Score that predicts renewal, churn, or growth | You rank accounts for attention | core |
| RAG (red, amber, green) status | Simple color rating of account health | You report account risk | core |
| Leading indicator | Signal that predicts a later result such as churn | You build early warning | expert |
| At-risk account | Account that shows signs it may churn | You start a save play | core |
| Playbook | Set steps a CSM runs when a trigger fires | You standardize CSM response | core |
| CTA (call to action) | Logged CSM task that a risk or event creates | You track follow-up work | expert |
| Cadence call | Regular, scheduled check-in with the customer | You keep engagement steady | core |
| QBR (quarterly business review) | Quarterly meeting on value delivered, results, and plans | You show value to the customer | core |
| EBR (executive business review) | Strategic review with customer executives | You secure executive support | expert |
| Executive sponsor | Senior customer leader who backs the program | You reduce single-contact risk | expert |
| Customer journey (lifecycle stages) | Defined stages a customer moves through | You map touchpoints and owners | core |
| Value realization | Customer actually gets the promised business results | You prove ROI | expert |

### Retention, renewals, and expansion

| Term | Plain meaning | Use it when | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Churn | Customers or revenue lost when they leave | You measure retention | core |
| Logo churn | Share of customer accounts lost in a period | You count lost customers | core |
| Revenue churn (gross dollar churn) | Recurring revenue lost to cancellations and downgrades | You measure revenue loss | core |
| Contraction (downsell) | Revenue drop from a smaller plan or fewer seats | You track partial loss | core |
| GRR (gross revenue retention) | Starting revenue kept after churn and downgrades, no expansion | You judge pure retention | core |
| NRR (net revenue retention) | Starting revenue kept, plus expansion, minus churn and downgrades | You judge growth from existing customers | core |
| ARR (annual recurring revenue) | Yearly value of all active subscriptions | You report the revenue base | core |
| Expansion revenue | New revenue from existing customers | You plan account growth | core |
| Upsell / cross-sell | Sell a higher plan, more units, or another product | You grow an existing account | core |
| Renewal | Customer signs for another contract term | You plan for contract end | core |
| ATR (available to renew) | Contract value up for renewal in a period | You forecast renewals | expert |
| Renewal rate | Renewed value divided by value up for renewal | You measure renewal results | core |
| Auto-renewal | Contract renews unless the customer cancels | You set renewal terms | core |
| Save play | Planned steps to keep a customer who wants to leave | You respond to cancel risk | expert |
| Churn reason codes | Standard categories for why customers left | You find causes of churn | expert |
| Customer advocacy | Customers who refer, review, or speak for you | Sales needs references and proof | expert |
| Concentration risk (revenue concentration) | Large share of revenue sits in a few accounts | You set coverage and risk plans for top accounts | expert |

### Support operations

| Term | Plain meaning | Use it when | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ticket (case) | Record of one customer request and its history | You track support work | core |
| Ticket status | Stage of a ticket, such as new, open, pending, solved | You design workflows | core |
| Queue | List of tickets that wait for one team | You route work | core |
| Triage | Sort tickets by type, priority, and owner | You handle inbound volume | core |
| Priority (severity) | Rank of urgency and business impact | You set the order of work | core |
| Routing (skills-based routing) | Send each ticket to an agent with the right skills | You cut transfers | expert |
| Tiered support (Tier 1, 2, 3) | Support levels by skill and issue complexity | You design escalation paths | core |
| Escalation | Move a ticket to more skill or authority | The frontline cannot resolve it | core |
| Incident | Unplanned outage or drop in service quality | Something is broken | core |
| Major incident | Severe incident that needs a coordinated response | Many customers are affected | expert |
| Problem (root cause) | Underlying cause of one or more incidents | You stop repeat incidents | expert |
| Service request | Routine ask that is not a failure, like access | You separate asks from breakages | expert |
| Macro (canned response) | Saved reply and ticket actions applied in one click | You speed up common answers | core |
| Backlog | Open tickets not yet resolved | You judge team load | core |
| Omnichannel support | Email, chat, phone, and social support in one view | You plan support channels | core |
| WFM (workforce management) | Forecast ticket volume and schedule agents to match | You staff the support team | expert |

### Support metrics and SLAs

| Term | Plain meaning | Use it when | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| SLA (service level agreement) | Promised response and resolution times for customers | You set service commitments | core |
| OLA (operational level agreement) | Internal agreement between teams that supports the SLA | Several teams work one ticket | expert |
| SLA breach | Missed SLA target on a ticket | You report service failures | core |
| FRT (first response time) | Time from ticket creation to first agent reply | You measure responsiveness | core |
| Next reply time | Time from oldest unanswered customer message to agent reply | You track responsiveness after the first reply | expert |
| Resolution time (time to resolution) | Time from ticket creation until it is solved | You measure speed to fix | core |
| FCR (first contact resolution) | Share of issues solved in the first contact | You judge efficiency and experience | core |
| One-touch resolution | Ticket solved with a single agent reply | You measure simple-ticket efficiency | expert |
| AHT (average handle time) | Average agent work time per contact | You staff phone or chat teams | core |
| Reopen rate | Share of solved tickets that customers reopen | You check fix quality | expert |
| Ticket volume (contact rate) | Number of tickets per period or per customer | You forecast staffing | core |
| CSAT (customer satisfaction score) | Rating a customer gives after an interaction | You measure service quality | core |
| CES (customer effort score) | How easy the customer found getting help | You find friction | expert |
| QA score (quality assurance) | Internal review score of how well an agent handled tickets | You coach agents | expert |

### Knowledge base and self-service

| Term | Plain meaning | Use it when | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Knowledge base (KB) | Library of help articles | You support agents and self-service | core |
| Help center | Customer-facing site with articles and contact forms | You launch self-service | core |
| Self-service | Customers solve issues without an agent | You cut ticket volume | core |
| Deflection rate | Share of issues kept away from human agents | You judge self-service impact | core |
| Automated resolution rate | Share of conversations fully solved by a bot | You judge a chatbot or AI agent | expert |
| KCS (Knowledge-Centered Service) | Method where agents write knowledge while they solve tickets | You build a KB that stays current | expert |
| Solve loop / evolve loop | KCS: capture during work, then improve from patterns | You design KB workflows | expert |
| Flag it or fix it | KCS rule: correct or report any wrong article | You keep the KB accurate | expert |
| Article link rate | Share of tickets linked to a KB article | You measure KB use | expert |

### Voice of the customer

| Term | Plain meaning | Use it when | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| VoC (voice of the customer) | Program to collect and act on customer feedback | You inform product or service changes | core |
| NPS (Net Promoter Score) | Percent promoters minus percent detractors | You track loyalty | core |
| Promoters, passives, detractors | NPS groups that score 9-10, 7-8, and 0-6 | You segment survey results | core |
| Relationship vs transactional survey | Periodic survey versus survey after one interaction | You choose a survey type | expert |
| Closed-loop feedback | Follow up with each respondent and act on their input | You run NPS or CSAT programs | expert |
| Inner loop / outer loop | Frontline follow-up versus company-wide root-cause fixes | You design a feedback system | expert |
| Contact driver (contact reason) | Main reason customers get in touch | You cut avoidable contacts | expert |
| Feature request | Customer ask for new product capability | You route feedback to product | core |
| Sentiment analysis | Automated reading of tone in customer text | You scan large volumes of feedback | expert |

## Deliverable shapes

| Deliverable | Standard parts |
|---|---|
| Onboarding plan | handoff notes, kickoff agenda, milestones to go-live, owners on both sides, TTV target, training plan, success criteria |
| Success plan | customer goals and desired outcomes, success metrics, milestones, owners, risks, review dates |
| QBR deck | goals recap, value delivered with metrics, adoption and usage, support summary, roadmap fit, next-quarter plan, asks |
| Health score model | goal, inputs and weights (usage, support, sentiment, outcomes), thresholds, RAG mapping, triggered playbooks, review cadence |
| Renewal forecast and risk review | ATR by period, forecast by account, risk level and reason, mitigation plan, expansion upside |
| Churn analysis | period and cohort, logo and revenue churn, GRR and NRR, churn reason codes, leading indicators, actions |
| Support SLA policy | priority definitions, response and resolution targets per priority, business hours, escalation rules, breach handling |
| Escalation process | triggers, tiers and owners, handoff fields, communication cadence, customer updates, post-incident review |
| Knowledge article (KCS) | title in customer words, issue, environment, resolution, cause, links, owner and review date |
| Support metrics dashboard | ticket volume, backlog, FRT, resolution time, FCR, reopen rate, CSAT, SLA attainment, contact drivers |
| VoC report | sources and sample, NPS or CSAT trend, top themes, contact drivers, verbatims, actions and owners, closed-loop status |
| At-risk account save plan | account and ARR at risk, renewal date, risk signals and health score, root cause, stakeholder map and executive sponsor, save actions with owners and dates, offer or concessions, success criteria, escalation path |
| Customer recovery letter | plain statement of the failure, ownership and apology, what went wrong, fix made, steps to prevent a repeat, goodwill gesture if any, named contact, next check-in date |

## Misused or dated terms

| Term | Problem | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| Customer service (for customer success) | Service reacts to issues; success drives outcomes before issues arise | customer support or customer success, by meaning |
| Churn rate (with no basis) | Unclear if it counts accounts or revenue | logo churn or revenue churn |
| NRR and GRR used as one metric | GRR excludes expansion; NRR includes it | name both, with the formula |
| Response time (alone) | Unclear if it means first reply, next reply, or resolution | FRT, next reply time, or resolution time |
| Deflection as resolution | A deflected customer may have given up unsolved | resolution rate, checked with CSAT |
| Closed ticket (for solved) | In many tools, solved and closed are separate statuses | solved, or closed, by tool status |
| Health score as usage only | Usage alone misses sentiment, support, and outcomes | weighted health score with several inputs |
| CSAT as a loyalty measure | CSAT rates one interaction, not the relationship | NPS or a relationship survey |
