# E-commerce and retail

E-commerce and retail covers how a brand or retailer lists, prices, sells, ships, and restocks physical products online and in stores. Store owners, e-commerce managers, merchandisers, buyers and planners, retention marketers, and operations leads use these terms.

## Terms

### Catalog and merchandising

| Term | Plain meaning | Use it when | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| SKU (stock keeping unit) | Unique code for one sellable version of a product | You track stock or list products | core |
| Variant | One combination of option values, like small and blue | You set up sizes, colors, or materials | core |
| Collection | Group of products shown on one browse page | You organize the store | core |
| Automated collection | Collection filled by rules such as tag, type, or price | You keep large catalogs current | expert |
| Product taxonomy | Category tree that classifies every product | You build navigation, filters, and feeds | expert |
| Metafields (custom product attributes) | Extra structured fields stored on products or variants | You add specs, filters, or size data | expert |
| PIM (product information management) | System that holds master product data for all channels | You manage a large or multichannel catalog | expert |
| Product feed | Data file of products sent to ads or marketplaces | You run Shopping ads or list on marketplaces | core |
| GTIN (global trade item number) | Global barcode number, such as a UPC or EAN | You list on Google or marketplaces | expert |
| Faceted navigation | Filters by attribute on collection or search pages | You help shoppers narrow choices | core |
| Assortment planning | Choosing which products, breadth, and depth to carry | You plan a season or a range | expert |
| Hero SKU | Top product that drives a large share of sales | You focus ads, stock, and page space | core |
| Product bundle | Several products sold together as one offer | You raise order value or move slow stock | core |

### Conversion and on-site CRO

| Term | Plain meaning | Use it when | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| CVR (conversion rate) | Share of sessions that end in an order | You judge store performance | core |
| CRO (conversion rate optimization) | Test-driven work to raise the conversion rate | You plan site changes and tests | core |
| PDP (product detail page) | Page for one product with its buy button | You improve product pages | core |
| PLP (product listing page) | Page that lists many products, like a collection | You improve browsing | core |
| AOV (average order value) | Revenue divided by number of orders | You work on basket size | core |
| RPV (revenue per visitor) | Revenue divided by visitors or sessions | You judge a test that moves CVR and AOV | expert |
| Add-to-cart rate | Share of sessions that add an item to the cart | You look for product page problems | core |
| Checkout conversion rate | Share of started checkouts that become orders | You fix checkout friction | core |
| Cart abandonment rate | Share of carts left without an order | You size recovery work | core |
| Guest checkout | Buying without creating an account | You cut checkout friction | core |
| Express checkout (accelerated checkout) | Wallet buttons that prefill payment and shipping details | You cut checkout steps, mostly on mobile | core |
| Unexpected costs (surprise costs) | Shipping, tax, or fees first shown late in checkout | You audit why shoppers abandon checkout | core |
| Form field count | Number of fields a shopper must fill to order | You shorten checkout forms | expert |
| Address autocomplete | Suggests full addresses as the shopper types | You speed address entry and cut typos | expert |
| Inline validation | Flags field errors while the shopper fills the form | You cut form errors and failed submits | expert |
| Trust signals | Badges, policies, and cues that make paying feel safe | You fix low trust at checkout | core |
| Payment methods | Ways a shopper can pay, such as cards, wallets, BNPL | You check if missing options lose orders | core |
| BNPL (buy now, pay later) | Pay in installments, often four, chosen at checkout | You offer financing to lift AOV or conversion | core |
| Upsell and cross-sell | Offer a better version, or add related items | You raise AOV | core |
| Free shipping threshold | Order value that unlocks free shipping | You raise AOV | core |
| Social proof | Ratings, reviews, and customer photos that build trust | You lift PDP conversion | core |
| Conversion funnel | Steps from visit to cart to checkout to order | You find where shoppers drop off | core |

### Retention and LTV

| Term | Plain meaning | Use it when | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| LTV (customer lifetime value) | Total revenue or margin a customer brings over time | You set how much to pay per customer | core |
| LTV:CAC ratio | Lifetime value divided by customer acquisition cost | You check unit economics | core |
| Repeat purchase rate | Share of customers who order more than once | You judge retention | core |
| Second-order rate | Share of first-time buyers who order again | You find the main retention gap | expert |
| Cohort retention | Repeat buying of customers grouped by first-order month | You track LTV curves over time | core |
| Time between orders | Typical days between one customer's orders | You time replenishment messages | expert |
| RFM (recency, frequency, monetary) | Scores customers by last order, order count, and spend | You segment customers by value | expert |
| Abandoned cart flow | Messages sent after a shopper leaves a cart | You recover lost checkouts | core |
| Browse abandonment flow | Messages after a shopper views products but adds none | You recover high-intent visitors | expert |
| Post-purchase flow | Messages after an order: thanks, how-to, review request | You lift reviews and repeat orders | core |
| Replenishment flow | Reminder timed to when the product runs out | You drive reorders of consumables | expert |
| Win-back flow | Messages to customers who have stopped buying | You recover lapsed customers | core |
| Subscription (subscribe and save) | Repeat orders shipped at a set interval | You build recurring revenue | core |
| Loyalty program | Points or tiers that reward repeat buying | You raise purchase frequency | core |

### Pricing, promotions, and margin

| Term | Plain meaning | Use it when | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| COGS (cost of goods sold) | Direct cost of the products that you sold | You compute margin | core |
| Landed cost | Unit cost plus freight, duty, and import fees | You price imported goods | expert |
| Gross margin | Revenue minus COGS, as a share of revenue | You judge product profit | core |
| Contribution margin | Revenue minus all variable costs, such as shipping and ads | You decide if a sale or ad pays | core |
| Markup | Amount added to cost to reach the selling price | You set retail prices | core |
| Keystone pricing | Retail price set at double the wholesale cost | You price for wholesale and retail | expert |
| MSRP (manufacturer's suggested retail price) | Price the brand recommends that retailers charge | You set list prices | core |
| MAP (minimum advertised price) | Lowest price a reseller may show in ads | You protect price across retailers | expert |
| Compare-at price | Original price shown struck through next to sale price | You show a discount on the page | core |
| Markdown | Permanent price cut to clear stock | You clear slow or seasonal inventory | core |
| Discount code | Code that applies a discount at checkout | You run a targeted promotion | core |
| BXGY (buy X get Y) | Free or cheaper item when buying another | You move units or bundle products | core |
| Tiered discount | Bigger discount at higher spend or quantity | You raise AOV in a sale | expert |
| Promo depth | Size of a discount as a share of price | You weigh promo cost against volume | expert |
| Price elasticity | How much demand changes when price changes | You test or plan a price move | expert |
| BFCM (Black Friday Cyber Monday) | Peak sale weekend in late November | You plan peak season | core |

### Inventory and retail metrics

| Term | Plain meaning | Use it when | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sell-through rate | Units sold divided by units received in a period | You judge if a product is selling | core |
| Inventory turnover (turns) | Times stock sells and is replaced in a period | You judge inventory efficiency | core |
| GMROI (gross margin return on inventory investment) | Gross margin earned per dollar of average inventory cost | You compare categories or products | expert |
| Weeks of supply | How long current stock lasts at the current sales rate | You plan reorders | core |
| Stockout (OOS, out of stock) | Item has no sellable units left | You track lost sales | core |
| Reorder point | Stock level that triggers a new purchase order | You set buying rules | core |
| Safety stock | Extra stock held against swings in demand or supply | You cut stockout risk | core |
| Lead time | Days from purchase order to stock received | You plan purchases | core |
| MOQ (minimum order quantity) | Smallest order a supplier will accept | You negotiate with suppliers | core |
| Demand forecast | Estimate of future unit sales per SKU | You plan buys and production | core |
| OTB (open-to-buy) | Budget left to buy new inventory in a period | You control buying spend | expert |
| Dead stock | Inventory that has not sold for a long time | You decide to mark down or liquidate | core |
| ABC analysis | Rank SKUs by their share of sales or margin | You focus stock and attention | expert |
| Shrink | Inventory loss from theft, damage, or error | You audit stock in stores or warehouses | expert |

### Fulfillment and returns

| Term | Plain meaning | Use it when | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3PL (third-party logistics) | Outside company that stores, packs, and ships orders | You outsource fulfillment | core |
| Fulfillment center | Warehouse that ships orders to customers | You plan logistics | core |
| Pick and pack | Pull ordered items from shelves and pack them | You price or audit fulfillment | core |
| Kitting | Combining several items into one SKU before shipping | You sell bundles or gift sets | expert |
| ASN (advance shipping notice) | Notice to the warehouse of incoming stock | You send inventory to a 3PL | expert |
| DIM weight (dimensional weight) | Billable weight based on package size | You cut shipping costs | expert |
| Shipping zones | Distance bands that set carrier rates | You place inventory or set rates | expert |
| Fulfillment SLA (service level agreement) | Promised time from order to shipment | You hold a 3PL to targets | core |
| Last-mile delivery | Final leg from local hub to the customer's door | You set the delivery promise | core |
| Dropshipping | Supplier ships to the customer; you hold no stock | You choose a fulfillment model | core |
| RMA (return merchandise authorization) | Approval and ID number for a customer return | You run returns | core |
| Return rate | Share of units or sales that come back | You judge product fit and cost | core |
| Reverse logistics | Moving returned goods back and processing them | You plan returns operations | expert |
| Disposition | Decision on a return: restock, refurbish, or liquidate | You process returned items | expert |
| Return fraud and wardrobing | Fake returns, or using an item then returning it | You set return policy | expert |

### Channels, marketplaces, and wholesale

| Term | Plain meaning | Use it when | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|
| DTC (direct-to-consumer) | Brand sells straight to buyers, not through retailers | You plan channel strategy | core |
| Omnichannel | One shopping experience across stores, web, and app | You join online and offline sales | core |
| BOPIS (buy online, pick up in store) | Order online, collect the order at a store | You link stores and web | core |
| Marketplace | Third-party site where many sellers list products | You add channels such as Amazon | core |
| Featured Offer (Buy Box) | Default seller offer on an Amazon product page | You compete on Amazon | expert |
| FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon) | Amazon stores, packs, and ships your marketplace orders | You choose Amazon fulfillment | expert |
| Retail media | Ads sold by retailers on their own sites and apps | You buy ads on Amazon or Walmart | expert |
| Wholesale | Selling in bulk to retailers at a lower price | You open retail accounts | core |
| Line sheet | Wholesale catalog with SKUs, prices, and minimums | You pitch products to retailers | core |
| Net terms (net 30, net 60) | Buyer pays the invoice within a set number of days | You set wholesale payment terms | core |
| Sell-in and sell-out | Units sold to retailers, and units retailers sell | You judge wholesale health | expert |
| EDI (electronic data interchange) | Standard data exchange of orders and invoices with retailers | You sell to large retailers | expert |

## Phrasebook

| Generic words | Trade words |
|---|---|
| more sales from the product page | lift PDP conversion rate |
| get people to spend more per order | raise AOV (average order value) |
| people leave without buying | high cart abandonment or checkout drop-off |
| get people to buy again | raise repeat purchase rate and second-order rate |
| how much a customer is worth | customer lifetime value (LTV) |
| different sizes and colors | variants, each with its own SKU |
| product categories on the site | collections and product taxonomy |
| recommend other products | cross-sell and upsell modules |
| emails for people who left their cart | abandoned cart flow |
| customers who have not bought in a while | lapsed customers; win-back flow |
| sale or discount | promotion (amount off, BXGY, tiered) or markdown |
| profit after shipping and ads | contribution margin per order |
| real cost of the product once it arrives | landed cost |
| other sites sell it too cheap | MAP (minimum advertised price) violation |
| products that do not sell | slow movers, dead stock, low sell-through |
| how fast stuff sells | sell-through rate and inventory turns |
| is our inventory earning its keep | GMROI |
| we ran out | stockout (OOS) |
| how much stock to order | reorder point and safety stock from a demand forecast |
| how much we can spend on stock | open-to-buy (OTB) |
| a company that ships orders for us | 3PL (third-party logistics) |
| big boxes cost too much to ship | DIM weight pricing |
| handle returns | returns process with RMA and disposition rules |
| people wear it then return it | wardrobing, return fraud |
| sell to shops | wholesale with a line sheet, MOQ, and net terms |
| sell on Amazon | marketplace channel; win the Featured Offer, choose FBA or self-fulfill |

## Deliverable shapes

| Deliverable | Standard parts |
|---|---|
| PDP brief | product title, price and compare-at price, variant selector, image and video set, key benefits, specs, size or fit guide, reviews, shipping and returns info, cross-sell module, primary CTA |
| CRO test plan | hypothesis, page and element, variants, primary metric (CVR or RPV), guardrail metrics (AOV, margin), traffic split, sample size, run time, decision rule |
| Promotion plan | dates, offer mechanic, eligible SKUs and exclusions, promo depth, margin impact, inventory check, channels, success metrics |
| Assortment plan | category, SKU count, breadth and depth, price points, hero SKUs, buy quantities, OTB, sell-through targets |
| Inventory plan | SKU, demand forecast, on hand, on order, lead time, safety stock, reorder point, weeks of supply |
| Retention flow map | flows (welcome, abandoned cart, browse abandonment, post-purchase, replenishment, win-back), trigger, timing, exit condition, offer rules |
| Returns policy | return window, eligible items, condition rules, refund, exchange, or store credit, who pays return shipping, RMA steps, exceptions |
| 3PL SLA and fee schedule | receiving time, order cut-off, ship-time SLA, pick accuracy, packaging specs, kitting, returns handling, fees (pick, storage, shipping) |
| Wholesale line sheet | product images, SKU, description, wholesale price, MSRP, MOQ, case pack, delivery window, payment terms |
| Weekly trading report | sessions, CVR, AOV, revenue, new and returning customers, CAC, MER, contribution margin, top SKUs, stock alerts, sell-through |
| Unit economics model | price, COGS, landed cost, fulfillment and payment fees, return cost, CAC, contribution margin, LTV, payback period |
| Checkout audit | step count, form field count, guest checkout, express checkout, total cost shown early (no unexpected costs), payment methods and BNPL, address autocomplete, inline validation and error messages, trust signals, mobile layout, drop-off by checkout step |

## Misused or dated terms

| Term | Problem | Use instead |
|---|---|---|
| Margin and markup (as one thing) | Margin divides by price; markup divides by cost | Name the one you mean, with its base |
| Conversion rate (with no base) | Sessions, visitors, and users give different rates | State the base, for example orders per session |
| Promo success (as revenue) | Revenue hides the margin given away | Contribution margin and incremental units |
| LTV (on revenue only) | Overstates what you can pay to acquire | Gross-margin LTV |
| Inventory turnover (retail sales over cost stock) | Mixes retail and cost values | COGS divided by average inventory at cost |
| Buy Box | Amazon renamed it | Featured Offer |
